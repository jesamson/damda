The Damda Plan

Website · Shopify · DoorDash · Grubhub

What We're Building

Three connected pieces make up the online side of the business:

  • Website — already built. Will be updated to connect to Shopify for product orders.
  • Shopify Store — handles online payments, orders, and inventory (coffee, home goods, merch).
  • DoorDash & Grubhub — delivery apps for café menu items, run separately from the store.

Each system runs as its own account with its own login and fees. My role is to build, connect, and maintain all three — not own any of them.

What I Need to Get Started

  • Legal business name, EIN, and business address
  • Business bank account (for payouts from Shopify, DoorDash, and Grubhub)
  • Business email — I can help set one up if needed
  • Full menu and product list with prices and photos
  • Business license / food handling permit info if required by delivery platforms

How Setup Works

  1. Accounts — You sign up for Shopify, DoorDash, and Grubhub using your business info. About 15–20 minutes per platform. I can walk you through each one on a screen share.
  2. Access — Once accounts exist, you send me a collaborator invite so I can start building without your password.
  3. Build & Connect — I connect the website to Shopify checkout and set up your menu, photos, and pricing on DoorDash and Grubhub.
  4. Review & Launch — We go through everything together before anything goes live.
