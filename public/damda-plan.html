The Damda Plan

Website · Shopify · DoorDash · Grubhub

01

What We're Building

Three connected pieces make up the online side of the business:

  • Website — the public face of the brand (already built), which will be updated to connect to Shopify for orders.
  • Shopify Store — handles online orders, payments, and inventory for products (coffee, home goods, merch).
  • DoorDash & Grubhub — separate delivery apps for food/drink items, connected to the café's day-to-day menu.

These three systems talk to each other but are run as separate accounts, each with its own login, fees, and settings

02

03

What Need To Get Started

  • Legal business name, EIN, and business address
  • A business bank account for payouts (Shopify, DoorDash, and Grubhub all pay out here)
  • A business email
  • A full menu / product list with prices and photos, for both the Shopify store and the delivery apps
  • Business license / food handling permit info, if required for delivery platforms in our city
